My story

I didn't set out to become a "digital wellness expert."

I set out to understand why my toddler was crying when I took the iPad away. And why I felt guilty every time I handed it to her in the first place. And why every parenting book seemed to offer the same advice: "Just limit screen time."

As if it were that simple.

Before Digital Guardian, I built a career evaluating the quality of media creative—analyzing how content is designed, structured, and optimized to capture human attention. I understood the psychology of persuasion. I knew what a "dark pattern" looked like before it had a name.

Then I became a parent. And I watched those same techniques—the infinite scroll, the variable rewards, the autoplay—target my child.

I realized that most parents are fighting this battle with one hand tied behind their backs. We're told to fear technology, but not how it works. We're shamed for our kids' screen time, but not given tools that actually fit real family life.

So I built what I wished I'd had: a resource hub that treats parents like intelligent adults and children like whole humans.

What I believe

Technology is not the enemy. Disconnection is.

I'm not here to help you eliminate screens. I'm here to help you eliminate the anxiety around them—by building the kind of relationship with your kids where you can talk about what they see online, set boundaries that stick, and model the digital habits you want them to inherit.

  • Research-backed strategies from the American Academy of Pediatrics and University of Michigan
  • Real-world tools tested in my own home, not theory from a textbook
  • Empathetic storytelling that meets parents where they are
  • A bridge between media expertise and lived parenting experience

And through workshops, consulting, and speaking engagements, I partner with schools, organizations, and corporate teams who understand that digital wellness isn't a parenting trend—it's a public health imperative.
